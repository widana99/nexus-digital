# Visual Design & Frontend Engineering Rules

## Brand Essence & Identity (PT Nexus Digital Strategy)
- Reference: `design.md` and `nexus-prd-v2.2.md`.
- Vibe: **Quiet confidence**, high-level institutional strategy, executive dignity, editorial refinement.
- NOT: A playful consumer app, gaming hub, dark-mode hacker terminal, or generic template SaaS.

## Typography & Proportions
- **Display / Headlines**: *Newsreader* (editorial serif, high-contrast, elegant, conveys authority).
- **Body / Interface**: *Schibsted Grotesk* (neutral, modern, legible, precise).
- **Technical & Tabular**: *IBM Plex Mono* (code, timestamps, tabular figures, badges, reference IDs).
- Fluid type scale with deliberate hierarchy; do not stack 5 font sizes in one card.

## Color Palette & Visual Construction
- **Single Accent Family**: Deep Navy (`#031F44`).
- **Base Surfaces**: Paper (`#FBFBF9`) and Ivory (`#F5F4F0`).
- **Separation & Borders**: Crisp 1px hairlines (`#E2E0D8` or translucent dark lines) instead of blurry drop shadows.
- Whitespace is treated as a core structural element, giving strategic air to headlines and editorial sections.

## Accessibility & Mobile Standards (WCAG AA)
- Normal text contrast >= 4.5:1; large text contrast >= 3:1.
- Visible focus rings for keyboard users (`Tab`, `Shift+Tab`).
- Full keyboard operability (Escape closes modals and menus, Enter/Space triggers actions).
- Mobile layout: Perfect reflow on screens from 375px upward; no horizontal scrolling; minimum touch target size 44px x 44px.
